WHY THIS WAY
The approach
Circuit training and muscle-building can work together, but there's a real constraint worth naming: at 45 minutes and a 25 lb dumbbell cap, straight heavy-rest strength work isn't the most efficient path to growth. So this plan runs density-based hypertrophy circuits — 10–15 reps, 30–45 seconds rest, push and pull movements paired so one muscle recovers while the other works.

The real trick
Once 25 lb dumbbells stop being the hard part, overload comes from three other levers — a resistance band layered on, a slower tempo, or less rest — not just heavier weight.
