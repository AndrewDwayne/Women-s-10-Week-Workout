# 10-Week Muscle-Building Program (Full Schedule) **Goal:** Build muscle | **Location:** Home | **Style:** Circuit | **Frequency:** 3x/week, 45 min | **Equipment:** Dumbbells (up to 25 lb) + resistance bands | **Cardio:** Treadmill This is the fully expanded version — every week below has its own rounds, reps, rest, and RPE targets spelled out, plus exactly which exercise picks up a band or tempo change that week. No "apply the rule yourself" — just read the week and go. --- ## Why band + tempo, not just weight At a 25 lb dumbbell cap, straight weight progression runs out fast. Once an exercise hits 2+ reps in reserve for two sessions in a row, load gets added back three ways instead: a resistance band looped on for extra tension, a slower 3-1-1 tempo (3s down, 1s pause, 1s up), or trimmed rest to raise density. Each week below tells you exactly which lever to pull and where. ## Day Templates (same exercises every week — only the numbers change) | | Day 1 — Lower Body + Horizontal Push-Pull | Day 2 — Upper Body + Core | Day 3 — Full Body: Single-Leg, Carry, Rotation | |---|---|---|---| | **Warm-Up** | 2 min brisk treadmill walk → bodyweight squats x10, glute bridges x10, arm circles x10, band pull-aparts x15 | 2 min jumping jacks or march in place → band external rotations x15, arm swings, cat-cow x10 | 2 min brisk walk → walking lunges x10, torso twists x10/side, band pull-aparts x15 | | **Exercises** | Goblet Squat (DB) · DB Romanian Deadlift · DB Floor/Bench Press · Band or DB Row | DB Shoulder Press · Band Lat Pulldown or DB Row · DB Lateral Raise · Plank | DB Reverse Lunge · DB Farmer's Carry · Band Woodchop · DB Hip Thrust | | **Cool-Down** | Quad stretch, hamstring stretch, doorway chest stretch | Shoulder cross-body stretch, child's pose, seated spinal twist | Hip flexor stretch, glute stretch, deep breathing | --- ## Week 1 — Foundation **Rounds:** 3 | **Reps:** 12-15 (Plank 30s · Carry 30m) | **Rest (exercises):** 45s | **Rest (rounds):** 90s | **RPE:** 6-7 Baseline week — pick a dumbbell weight that hits the top of the rep range with 2-3 reps left in the tank on every exercise. No bands or tempo changes yet. **Cardio:** Day 1 — 10 min steady, moderate incline walk · Day 2 — 10 min intervals (1 min brisk/1 min easy x5) · Day 3 — 12 min steady, moderate pace. --- ## Week 2 — Foundation **Rounds:** 3 | **Reps:** 12-15 (Plank 35s · Carry 30-35m) | **Rest (exercises):** 40s | **Rest (rounds):** 85s | **RPE:** 6-7 If any exercise hit 2+ reps in reserve for two sessions running, bump that dumbbell weight. Everything else holds steady. **Cardio:** Same structure as Week 1, one notch further (slightly longer or slightly higher incline if it felt easy). --- ## Week 3 — Foundation **Rounds:** 3 | **Reps:** 12-15 (Plank 40s · Carry 35m) | **Rest (exercises):** 35s | **Rest (rounds):** 80s | **RPE:** 7 Add a 3-1-1 tempo to: **Row (Day 1)**, **Lateral Raise (Day 2)**, **Hip Thrust (Day 3)** — these usually hit the 25 lb ceiling first. **Cardio:** Day 1 — 10 min steady · Day 2 — 10 min intervals · Day 3 — 12 min steady (holding Week 1-2 structure). --- ## Week 4 — Overload **Rounds:** 3 | **Reps:** 10-12 (Plank 40s · Carry 35-40m) | **Rest (exercises):** 30s | **Rest (rounds):** 75s | **RPE:** 7-8 Loop a resistance band under your feet for **Squat & RDL (Day 1)** and **Shoulder Press (Day 2)**, and around your back for **Reverse Lunge (Day 3)** — dumbbell weight alone has likely capped out here. **Cardio:** Day 1 — 12 min steady, incline up one notch · Day 2 — 10 min intervals, higher incline · Day 3 — 14 min steady. --- ## Week 5 — Overload **Rounds:** 3 | **Reps:** 10-12 | **Rest (exercises):** 30s | **Rest (rounds):** 75s | **RPE:** 7-8 Add 3-1-1 tempo to **Squat + Bench Press (Day 1)** and **Row + Lat Pulldown (Day 2)**; slow the Carry pace and add tempo to the **Woodchop (Day 3)**. **Cardio:** Same as Week 4. --- ## Week 6 — Overload **Rounds:** Day 1: 4 · Day 2-3: 3 | **Reps:** 10-12 | **Rest (exercises):** 30s | **Rest (rounds):** 75s | **RPE:** 8 Day 1 picks up a 4th round. Layer a band onto **Row (Day 1)** and **Hip Thrust (Day 3)** on top of last week's tempo work. **Cardio:** Day 1 — 12 min steady · Day 2 — 10 min intervals · Day 3 — 14 min steady. --- ## Week 7 — Performance **Rounds:** Day 1-2: 4 · Day 3: 3 | **Reps:** 8-12 | **Rest (exercises):** 25s | **Rest (rounds):** 70s | **RPE:** 8 Day 2 picks up a 4th round. Combine band + tempo together on **Squat (Day 1)** and **Shoulder Press (Day 2)** — your hardest movements this week. **Cardio:** Day 1 — 15 min steady, higher incline · Day 2 — 12 min intervals (30s brisk/90s easy x6) · Day 3 — 15 min steady, moderate-hard pace. --- ## Week 8 — Performance **Rounds:** All days: 4 | **Reps:** 8-12 | **Rest (exercises):** 20-25s | **Rest (rounds):** 65s | **RPE:** 8 Day 3 picks up its 4th round. Combine band + tempo on **Reverse Lunge** and **Hip Thrust**. **Cardio:** Same as Week 7. --- ## Week 9 — Performance (Peak) **Rounds:** All days: 4 | **Reps:** 8-12 | **Rest (exercises):** 20s | **Rest (rounds):** 60s | **RPE:** 8-9 Peak week. Two exercises per day run combined band + tempo: **Squat + Press (Day 1)**, **Press + Row (Day 2)**, **Lunge + Hip Thrust (Day 3)**. **Cardio:** Same as Week 7-8. --- ## Week 10 — Deload & Testing **Rounds:** 2 | **Reps:** 10-12 (Plank 30s · Carry 30m) | **Rest (exercises):** 45s | **Rest (rounds):** 90s | **RPE:** 6 Strip every band and tempo modifier — back to plain dumbbell weight at Week 1 loads. Mid-week, retest max clean reps at your Week 1 baseline weight on one lift per day and log it against your original numbers. **Cardio:** All days — 10 min easy, flat/light incline only. --- ## Nutrition (General Principles Only) - **Protein:** ~0.7-1.0g per lb bodyweight/day — at 130-160 lb that's roughly 90-160g/day, lean toward the higher end for muscle building. - **Hydration:** ~0.5-1 oz per lb bodyweight daily. - **Pre-workout:** light carb + protein 1-2 hrs prior. - **Calories:** a small surplus, roughly 200-300 kcal above maintenance, supports growth without an aggressive bulk. ## Progress Tracking | Metric | How | |---|---| | Body weight | Weekly, same day/time | | Measurements | Waist, arms — weekly | | Strength | Log reps completed + RIR per exercise | | RPE / Energy / Sleep | Daily or per session | | Cardio | Pace / incline / duration | **Checkpoints:** Week 1 baseline · Week 5 mid-program check · Week 10 retest vs. baseline. ## Top 3 Rules 1. Progress via band/tempo/rest once dumbbells stop being hard — don't run the same 25 lb reps for 10 weeks. 2. Protein and sleep matter as much as the workout — muscle is built in recovery. 3. Show up 3x/week even on light days. ## Common Mistakes to Avoid - Sticking with 25 lb indefinitely without adding a band or tempo. - Rushing circuits so fast that form breaks down. - Under-eating protein while expecting muscle gain. - Skipping warm-up because the session is short. ## After Week 10 Compare retest numbers to Week 1. If dumbbells + bands stay easy even with tempo added, invest in heavier dumbbells or a stronger band for the next block. Otherwise, repeat this structure with fresh baselines, or shift toward lower reps/longer rest if pure muscle gain has plateaued.
